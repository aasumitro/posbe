### Menu Order

Menu orders can be displayed as either pages or modals. The modal can be accessed from table ordering, while the page view is typically used by the cashier for quick orders.

#### Add Product to Cart
1. Browse and filter products from the product list.
2. Click on a product to open the **Order Detail** modal.
3. Select the desired **product variant** (if available).
4. Add any **addons** to the product.
5. (Optional) Enter **notes** for the order.
6. Click **Save** or **Add to Cart** to confirm.

#### Update Product in Cart
1. In the **Cart** section, locate the product you want to edit.
2. Click the **Edit** button to open the **Order Detail** modal.
3. Modify the product’s variants, addons, or notes as needed.
4. Click **Update** to save changes.
5. If the updated product matches another item in the cart (same variant and addons), merge them and increase the quantity instead of creating a duplicate entry.
6.	If the notes are different, the items can still be merged, but the notes should be separated into two sections.
